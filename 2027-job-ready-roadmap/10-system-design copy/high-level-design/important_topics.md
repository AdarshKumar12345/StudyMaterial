# High-Level Design (HLD) - Study Topics

1. Monolithic vs. Microservices vs. Serverless Architectures
2. Load Balancing (L4 vs. L7, Round Robin, Least Connections, Hash-based)
3. Caching Strategies (Write-through, Write-behind, Cache-aside, Eviction Policies like LRU/LFU)
4. Database Selection (SQL vs. NoSQL: Key-Value, Document, Column-family, Graph)
5. Message Queues and Event Streaming (RabbitMQ, Apache Kafka, Pub/Sub)
6. API Gateways, Reverse Proxies, and Service Meshes
7. Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) and Edge Computing
8. Rate Limiting, Circuit Breakers, and Bulkhead Patterns
9. Disaster Recovery, Redundancy, and High Availability (Active-Active vs Active-Passive)
10. System Scalability (Vertical/Scale-up vs Horizontal/Scale-out)
