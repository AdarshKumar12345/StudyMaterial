# Quick Commerce Platform (Production Capstone) - Study & Implementation Topics

1. Inventory Management & Concurrent Flash Sales (Distributed Locks, Redis)
2. Geospatial Search and Routing (Redis GEO, PostGIS, S2 Geometry)
3. Distributed Order State Machine (Saga Pattern, Orchestration vs Choreography)
4. Idempotent Payment Processing (Stripe/Razorpay Integration, Webhooks)
5. Cart Management and Session Storage
6. Delivery Partner Matching and Dispatch Algorithms
7. Dynamic & Surge Pricing Architecture
8. Real-time Order Tracking (WebSockets, Kafka, Location Streaming)
9. Event Sourcing for Order History and Auditing
10. Multi-Service Microservices Architecture (Auth, Catalog, Cart, Order, Rider)
